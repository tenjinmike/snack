# Product strategy — draft 2026-11-07
## Purpose
A Korean platform for the single link in an Instagram bio, as an alternative to overseas link-in-bio products.
Customers: Korean creators, individual users, small shops and brands.
Japan is a later localization and market-validation track. Accommodation is not the primary target.

## Core value
Fast mobile editing, attractive public profiles, Korean onboarding/support, familiar local channel links, usable analytics.
Naver, Kakao, shopping and content channels should be easy to add; a stored URL alone is not a verified platform integration.
AI is an optional helper for descriptions and localization, not the prerequisite for the link service.

## Service model — user decision, 2026-11-07
The service is free to users. Profile creation, current themes, link editing and public sharing have no subscription or payment requirement. Do not add paid tiers, checkout, trials or card collection without a new product decision. Hosting and support remain operator costs. Advertising and sponsorship are undecided and have not been activated. AI is not implemented and no unlimited AI promise is made.

## Korea and Japan validation plan
Interview creators, shops and brands separately about current tool, migration friction, most-used channels, page editing, privacy, desired features.
Compare use cases and native Korean/Japanese wording. Do not infer SaaS demand only from travel statistics.
Target 10 interviews per market and a small pilot before targeting 30 pilot accounts; these are planned targets.
Measure activation, publish rate, repeat editing, link clicks, retention, support cost and usability.
Measure ad impressions and realized net RPM; compare against engagement/conversion loss.
Distinguish advertising revenue from the acquisition advertising budget.

## 2027 support-program preparation
BlueIT start date: 2024-06-01. Eligibility and funding depend on final applicant-company announcements.
Prepare genuine customer interviews, release history, pilot evidence, technical measurements, original methods and spend plans.
Do not describe API integration alone as proven deep-tech. No maximum award or selection is guaranteed.
Track software/business validation work separately from existing IT field-service work.
Business-sensitive application materials should be maintained privately, outside this public repository.
